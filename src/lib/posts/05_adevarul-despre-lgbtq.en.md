Hi! First of all, I want to say that, in the past, I wasn't a hater of the LGBTQ+ community, but I didn't support them either. I avoided having any connection with this subject simply because I didn't understand why these people are “that way”. Still, that doesn't mean I hated them, because I think it's in poor taste to hate someone without a concrete reason or without them having done you any harm. Over time, I started informing myself more in order to understand why some people are born gay, or why people are born boys or girls but, as their consciousness forms, realise they feel like the opposite gender or even neither of them.

<p class="fara-alineat">To understand this phenomenon, we have to start at the beginning: the development of the foetus in the womb. What gender does a foetus have at the start? None. It is a neutral being, with the chance to develop biologically either as a girl or as a boy. For example, this is why men have nipples too – in the early stages they could have become women, if the level of oestrogen in the body had exceeded that of testosterone. So, in the womb you start as a being with no defined gender. During development, hormones (oestrogen or testosterone) decide, in a first phase, the biological sex and the reproductive organs the body will have.</p>

<p class="fara-alineat">But once the hormones have formed the body, the brain develops only later. Here the great dilemma and the explanation appear: there is a chance that the hormones reaching and shaping the brain do not match the hormones that dictated the development of the reproductive organs. For example, if a person is born biologically male but realises he likes men, this happens because a discrepancy occurred. During the development of his brain, the level of oestrogen (the female hormone) was much higher than that of testosterone. Since women are (generally) attracted to men, the higher presence of this type of chemistry in his brain makes him attracted to men as well, regardless of his biological body. The brain is what controls everything. The same principle applies to women who realise they like women: their brains were exposed to a greater amount of testosterone than oestrogen.</p>

<p class="fara-alineat">Since we are talking about how the brain and biology dictate attraction, other orientations also fall within this spectrum:</p>

    **Bisexuality:** occurs when the brain is wired in such a way that the person feels attraction to both genders (to both men and women).

    **Pansexuality:** is when attraction transcends biological gender. The brain of a pansexual person is simply drawn to the other person's personality, soul or energy, regardless of whether that person is a man, a woman or transgender.

    **Asexuality:** happens when the chemistry that triggers sexual attraction is simply missing or manifests at an extremely low level. An asexual person can love romantically, but does not feel the need for the sexual impulse.

<p class="fara-alineat">Coming back to development, the same hormonal logic also explains the situation of transgender people. They are born biologically male or female, but because the brain developed later in a hormonal bath different from the body's, its chemistry does not match the anatomy. Since the brain is “the boss”, it will clearly say “I am a girl”, even if the body was born male.</p>

<p class="fara-alineat">So, is this an illness? No. It is a chemical difference that can simply happen because the development of the body and of the brain does not take place simultaneously, and there is a chance the hormones will not match. It is an uncontrollable process, and the people in this community do not deserve the hate of those who are misinformed and full of hatred. It is no one's fault that the biology of the brain decided they would be attracted to the same gender or identify differently.</p>

<p class="fara-alineat">Another fascinating thing I learned about the human body is directly related to transgender people and hormone treatments. Since at the beginning we are all neutral beings, if a biological boy starts oestrogen treatment, he has the ability to develop the traits of the opposite gender (breasts, changes in hair, skin, face). Hormones control the way we look. The fact that a boy can transform into a girl (or the other way around) by tampering with the body's chemistry, using the same methods that shaped us at birth, is something amazing, but also frightening at the same time. Why? Because it is an irreversible process. That is why transition should only be undertaken by people who truly suffer and do not feel at all right in their body. If a person who feels good with their gender plays with hormones just for the sake of trying it, that is very dangerous; they can turn their body upside down, especially if they want to return to its original form.</p>

<p class="fara-alineat">In conclusion, LGBTQ+ is nothing more than a biological reality. If you take the time to study and understand it, you realise for yourself how stupid it is to hate people just because they are different. I repeat: is it an illness? No. It is diversity. This phenomenon also appears in the animal world; the difference is that animals do not judge the way we do.</p>

<p class="fara-alineat">Is sex between two people of the same gender moral? Some would say that, biologically or strictly evolutionarily, it is not, because the primary purpose of sex is reproduction between two opposite genders. But as long as it harms no one, every person should be free to do as they wish in their intimate life. Here is society's great problem: people get stuck only on the idea of the sexual act and its morality. But the foreground is not sex; it is these people's desire to have the freedom to love the people they were (biologically) built to love. They want to be able to live happily, just like all of us, and they want society to accept diversity, to give up extreme traditionalism, ugly souls and misinformation.</p>

<p class="fara-alineat">Even when it comes to the Bible, if you analyse it, it does not necessarily forbid love between two people of the same sex, but condemns the sexual act itself, precisely on the grounds that it does not lead to reproduction. That's it.</p>

Keep in mind that this is still considered a theory in the medical world, called the Prenatal Hormone Exposure Theory, because what actually happens is much more complex and hard to determine exactly. I personally hope that in the future we will become even more advanced, so that we can find the answers to the questions that currently have no 100% confirmed and true answer. But what we do know is that this is the theory closest to the truth that can explain these phenomena, even if it does not apply to everyone.

This is my opinion, based on the study I have done on this subject. Be better people at heart! When you hate the people around you and judge them constantly, you do nothing but show what kind of soul you actually have.
